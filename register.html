Book Bank Usage Rules
1. Book Borrowing Limits

Students may hold a maximum of 2 active reservations at any given time.

2. Physical Collection Window

Once a reservation is approved by the admin, the physical book must be picked up within 48 hours, or the hold will expire.

3. Book Care & Return

Books must be returned in good condition by the specified due date to avoid borrowing privileges being suspended.
